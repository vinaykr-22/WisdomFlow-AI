**# Master Development Prompt**



**## Project Name**



**\*\*LearnFlow AI\*\***



**## Project Vision**



**Build an AI-powered personalized learning platform that transforms any document or learning topic into a complete learning experience instead of just providing summaries. The platform should act as a virtual teacher by understanding educational content, simplifying difficult concepts, generating structured learning roadmaps, explaining topics through AI voice conversations, and continuously evaluating the learner through quizzes and flashcards.**



**The system should combine document intelligence, conversational AI, voice interaction, adaptive learning, and progress tracking into one unified platform.**



**---**



**# Core Objectives**



**The application must allow users to:**



**1. Upload educational documents.**

**2. Extract and understand document content.**

**3. Generate structured summaries.**

**4. Identify key concepts.**

**5. Explain difficult topics.**

**6. Generate personalized learning paths.**

**7. Teach through AI voice conversations.**

**8. Answer follow-up questions using uploaded content.**

**9. Generate quizzes and flashcards.**

**10. Track learning progress.**

**11. Recommend revision schedules.**
